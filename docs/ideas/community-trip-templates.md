# Community trip templates

Status: future idea, captured 10 September 2026.

## Idea

Create a TripDock community library where people can publish trip templates and other people can replicate them as the starting point for their own trips.

## Desired experience

- Turn a trip into a reusable template and choose to publish it to the community library.
- Browse templates and preview their destinations and itinerary.
- Copy a template into a personal trip, then adapt its dates, destinations, transportation, stays, and activities.
- Treat the copied trip as independently editable; changes should not alter the original template.

## Questions for later planning

- Which trip fields belong in a template, and which personal details should be excluded?
- How should dates and itinerary timing adapt when a template is copied?
- What discovery, attribution, moderation, and template-update behavior is needed?

The public template library is separate from the [personal packing library](personal-packing.md), which should not be shared.
