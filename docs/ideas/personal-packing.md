# Personal packing and custom categories

Status: product direction, captured 10 September 2026, to carry into future packing work.

## Direction

Packing should be a local, personal feature. People should be able to create their own categories and organize their packing library and lists around their own needs. The packing library and lists should not be shared.

## Desired experience

- Create and customize personal packing categories and items.
- Maintain a personal organization system rather than relying only on fixed categories.
- Keep packing information private when sharing a trip, publishing a community template, or opening a guest view.

## Relationship to existing planning

A separate packing implementation brief already exists in the working context. This note records the user's explicit preference for future decisions: local, customizable, and unshared. It does not claim an implementation status or change that other brief.

## Question for later planning

Clarify whether "local" should remain tied to one installation or may eventually include private synchronization between the user's own devices. Cross-user sharing is outside this direction either way.
