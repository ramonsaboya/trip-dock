# Voice dictation for the AI composer

Status: future idea, captured 10 September 2026.

## Idea

Allow people to dictate into the AI chat or composer instead of typing. The interaction should be as simple as clicking to speak, with recognized words appearing smoothly while the person talks.

## Desired experience

- Start dictation with a clear microphone control in the composer.
- Show transcription progressively, with minimal delay and visual disruption as recognition updates.
- Make the listening state clear and provide an easy way to stop.
- Leave the transcribed text editable before submission.
- Keep ordinary text entry available alongside dictation.

## Questions for later planning

- Which browsers, devices, and languages should be supported first?
- How should interim transcription corrections behave so the text remains easy to read?
- How should dictation interact with existing typed text, pauses, and microphone errors?
- Which speech recognition approach meets the desired responsiveness and privacy expectations?
