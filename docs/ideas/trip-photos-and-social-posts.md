# Trip photos and social posts

Status: future idea, captured 10 September 2026.

## Idea

Make it easy to upload, organize, and share pictures for an entire trip, portions of a trip, and individual destinations. Activities, transportation, and stays should also support attached photo collections.

## Desired experience

- Manually choose pictures and attach them to the relevant trip, destination, activity, transportation leg, or stay.
- Optionally use geographical file metadata to suggest which trip or itinerary entry a photo belongs to. Capture date and time could help distinguish visits to the same place.
- Present suggestions such as "Here are the pictures for this trip. Would you like to upload them?" Let the user review the selection and approve the upload, then handle it automatically.
- Keep manual selection and matching available when metadata is missing or a suggestion is wrong.
- Make the resulting photo collections easy to share.
- Potentially generate an Instagram post about the trip from selected pictures, with a proposed selection, order, and caption for review.

## Questions for later planning

- Which photo sources should be supported first: selected files, a device photo library, or another service?
- How should photos linked to several itinerary entries appear without duplicate uploads?
- Who can view or contribute to shared collections, and how does this relate to the [guest trip view](guest-trip-view.md)?
- What location metadata should be retained or exposed when sharing?
- Should Instagram support begin with an exportable draft, or eventually include publishing after explicit approval?
