# Guest trip view

Status: future idea, captured 10 September 2026.

## Idea

Let someone share a trip they are planning with another person who only needs an overview. The recipient can view the plan without modifying it or becoming a collaborator.

## Desired experience

- Share access to a readable overview of the trip and its itinerary.
- Let the guest understand the destinations, activities, transportation, and stays in the plan.
- Keep guest access read-only, with no editing or collaboration capabilities.
- Keep personal packing information out of the guest view.
- Consider including selected shared photos when [trip photo collections](trip-photos-and-social-posts.md) are available.

## Questions for later planning

- Should access use a share link, a named invitation, or both?
- Should recipients see the current plan or a snapshot from when it was shared?
- Which details should the owner be able to include or hide?
- How should the owner revoke access or set an expiry?
