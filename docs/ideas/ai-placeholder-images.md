# AI placeholder images for trips and places

Status: future idea, captured 10 September 2026.

## Idea

Generate images with AI to fill visual placeholders for trips and places when suitable photos are not available.

## Desired experience

- Generate a relevant illustration for a trip or place from its context.
- Use it as a cover or placeholder until the user chooses another image or adds a real photo.
- Keep generated imagery distinguishable from actual travel photos.

## Questions for later planning

- Which surfaces should receive generated images first: trip covers, destination cards, or other itinerary entries?
- Should generation be requested by the user or offered when an image is missing?
- What visual style, generation cost, and storage approach fit the product?
- How should users replace or regenerate an image?
